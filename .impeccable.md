# Impeccable — Design Context for techbyjuan.com

## Design Context

### Users
Employers, recruiters, and engineering managers evaluating Juan for software roles (full-stack, AI, fintech-leaning). They arrive from LinkedIn/resume, skim on desktop and mobile, and decide within seconds whether he reads as senior and worth a conversation. Secondary audience: peers and potential collaborators.

### Brand Personality
Technical & precise, confident, well-rounded. Voice is direct and substantive — no fluff, lets the work speak. The interface should feel engineered and intentional, like a senior engineer who ships. Emotional goal: "this person is sharp and gets things done." Three words: **engineered, precise, confident.**

### Aesthetic Direction
**Light** — warm "engineering paper" (warm off-white in OKLCH, never pure white). **NOT navy/blue** (the rejected current palette) and **NOT** the cyan/purple AI cliché. Warm neutrals tinted toward the accent hue, with a **single warm copper accent** (~`oklch(0.58 0.115 50)`) used sparingly (~10%). Layout is structured and grid-driven with *visible* precision: indexed sections (01–04), hairline rules, monospace meta labels, asymmetric hero, generous spatial rhythm, low corner radius. Typography: **Bricolage Grotesque** (distinctive display) + **Hanken Grotesk** (clean grotesque body) + **JetBrains Mono** (technical/meta labels ONLY — never as lazy "tech" shorthand). Anti-references: generic dark+glow dev templates, endless identical card grids, gradient text, decorative glassmorphism, navy/cream.

### Design Principles
1. **Precision over decoration** — every rule, index number, and label is intentional; structure *is* the aesthetic.
2. **One accent, used rarely** — the copper accent earns attention because it's scarce (60-30-10 by visual weight).
3. **Show the work, not adjectives** — concrete projects and outcomes over buzzwords.
4. **Warm & controlled, never garish** — OKLCH with chroma reduced near white; no pure #000/#fff; neutrals tinted warm toward the copper hue.
5. **Motion with restraint** — one orchestrated load reveal, smooth ease-out (quart/expo); no bounce, no scattered micro-noise.
6. **Distinctive, not trendy** — must pass "how was this made?" not "which AI made this?"

### Constraints
Next.js (static export) + Tailwind, deploys to Cloudflare Pages (`techbyjuan`). Accessible: WCAG AA contrast on the light theme, full keyboard nav, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored. Feature: Trinity (personal product, no client/firm names), client builds as personal projects (Mia Fit, Vilar Safety, English Woods), Memex (open-source). Keep Vico OFF the site.

### Personal / Human
The site must feel **personal**, not a faceless résumé — Juan's explicit ask. Feature a real **portrait of Juan** (candidates in `~/Documents/Photos/`: `suit-headshot-0698-4x5.jpg` = navy suit + Colombian stole, professional; `headshot-2026-4x5.jpg` = casual/warm). Lean into his **Colombian-American** identity and a **first-person** voice with a little personality. This warmth is balanced *against* the precise/structured layout — the structure stays technical; the photo + voice add the human. HARD BOUNDARY: do NOT include any family/partner info, and never use the ID / passport / immigration images.
